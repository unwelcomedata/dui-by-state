## Day 1 — Bivariate map (consumption vs fatality)

**File:** `outputs/social/social_bivariate_consumption_fatality.png`
**Format:** X landscape (1600×900)

**Copy:**

> Does more drinking mean more DUI deaths?
>
> Not really. The correlation is almost zero.
>
> States with high alcohol consumption (teal) often have LOW DUI fatality rates. States with low consumption (coral) sometimes have the WORST rates.
>
> So what IS driving the difference?
>
> Data: NIAAA 2022, NHTSA FARS 2024, FHWA VMT
>
> #DUI #DataViz #TrafficSafety

**Alt text:** Bivariate choropleth of US states. X-axis: per-capita alcohol consumption. Y-axis: DUI fatality rate per 100 million vehicle miles traveled. No visible correlation. Teal states (high consumption, low fatalities) cluster in the upper Midwest and Northeast. Coral states (low consumption, high fatalities) cluster in the South.

**Alt text:** US choropleth map showing how many DUI convictions trigger a felony charge, by state. 3 jurisdictions (DC, MD, NJ) in gray have no felony DUI law. 4 states in coral become felony on 2nd offense. 24 states in teal on 3rd. 20 states in dark blue on 4th. Bar chart legend shows state counts per category.

## Day 3 — First-time vs repeat offenders

**File:** `outputs/social/social_first_time_vs_repeat.png`
**Format:** Instagram portrait (1080×1350)

**Copy:**

> 93% of DUI-related deaths involve a driver with NO prior DUI conviction.
>
> These aren't "repeat offenders." They've just never been caught — or their charges were pled down to something lesser.
>
> Felony escalation targets the 7%. What about the other 93%?
>
> Data: NHTSA FARS 2024 (PREV_DWI field)
>
> #DUI #DataViz #TrafficSafety #FARS

**Alt text:** Stacked horizontal bar chart. States are grouped by how many DUI convictions trigger a felony. In every group, 90-97% of impaired drivers in fatal crashes had no prior DUI conviction (teal). Only 3-10% had a prior conviction (coral). National average: 92.5% first-time, 7.5% repeat.

**Note:** This is portrait format (1080×1350). On X it will display cropped — consider posting with "click to expand" framing, or resize to landscape for X and keep portrait for Instagram if you add that later.

## Hashtags

Keep to 3-4 max per post:
- `#DUI` — primary topic
- `#DataViz` — attracts the data community
- `#TrafficSafety` — attracts policy/advocacy people
- `#FARS` — niche but gets NHTSA/researcher eyes on day 3

Don't use: `#data` (too generic), `#infographic` (wrong audience), `#alcohol` (attracts wrong crowd).

---

## Engagement strategy

- **Reply to your own thread** with a "methodology note" — shows rigor, attracts researchers
- **Quote-post** anyone who engages with a follow-up stat from the dataset
- **Pin** whichever post gets the most traction in the first 24 hours
- **Post times:** Weekday mornings (8-9am ET) for policy/professional audience, or evenings (7-9pm ET) for general audience. Test both.
